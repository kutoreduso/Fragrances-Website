History

Creed 1760

Established in 1760, the House of Creed transitioned from a London tailoring firm to a prestigious Parisian perfumery serving European royalty. After over 250 years as a family-run business famous for hand-blended fragrances, it was acquired by the luxury group Kering in 2023.

Jean Paul Gaultier

Jean Paul Gaultier launched its fragrance line in 1993 with the iconic "Classique," followed by the groundbreaking men's bestseller "Le Male" in 1995. Known for eccentric packaging and provocative marketing, the highly successful brand was acquired by the Spanish luxury group Puig in 2016.

Giorgio Armani

Giorgio Armani entered the fragrance market in the early 1980s, achieving massive global success with the 1996 release of the iconic aquatic scent "Acqua di Giò." Partnered with L'Oréal, the brand continues to mirror its elegant fashion aesthetic through best-selling lines like Armani Code and the exclusive Armani Privé collection.
